## 1. Asked for Gopi (QoreUps manager) to be put on Trioangle work

**Date:** ~May 2025 (approximate, from memory)  ·  **Logged:** 2026-09-21 — **~16 months after the event**
**Type:** Both — started official (resourcing), turned personal (dismissal)
**Entity:** Cross-entity — QoreUps resource pulled toward Trioangle
**Status:** Open

**What happened:**
Bowshul asked for **Gopi**, QoreUps's manager, to be assigned to Trioangle work — **permanently**, not on loan for a project.

> Read in light of entry #2: a permanent transfer of QoreUps's manager is not a resourcing request. It is the first move of a wind-down. See #2 before treating this entry as being about workload.

**What I said:**
If I give up Gopi, my own load goes *up*, not down. I am already carrying:
- Trioangle — full responsibility
- InnBlockchain — full responsibility
- QoreUps — full responsibility
- Salary / payroll issue
- Sales and marketing
- Chief technical review

**What he said:**
"Everyone is also working like you."
Sense of it: *you are doing work like everyone else, nothing is actually burdening you.*

**Why it matters:**
Two separate problems, and they should not be argued as one:

1. **Resourcing (official).** Moving Gopi to Trioangle takes capacity out of the entity I am covering alone and puts it into the entity Bowshul already covers. Net effect on my load is negative, not neutral.
2. **The dismissal (personal).** "Everyone is also working like you" flattens the difference between doing one entity's work and carrying three entities plus payroll, sales, and technical review. That is the part that triggered me — not the request itself.

**This is not a visibility gap — ruled out:**
All three companies run on the **same floor**, and Bowshul knows what I am responsible for. He is not missing information. He is minimising work he can see. Do not re-open "maybe he just doesn't know" in future entries.

**Still interpretation, not evidence:**
Whether the minimising was *considered* (he genuinely rates my load as ordinary) or *instrumental* (a line used to win the argument about Gopi) is not established. Both are bad; they need different responses.

**Outcome — I approved it:**
Gopi moved. Split **half day Trioangle / half day QoreUps**, still running that way. I objected, was dismissed, and then agreed anyway.

**What that outcome actually means:**
- QoreUps lost 50% of its manager. Trioangle gained it. Bowshul got most of what he asked for.
- I absorbed the insult *and* the capacity loss. Objecting cost me the argument and changed the result only from 100% to 50%.
- **Self-fulfilling risk:** QoreUps's weak revenue is now partly a consequence of resources taken out of it. If the closure case (#2) is built on QoreUps numbers, those numbers have been depressed by this transfer. Do not concede a revenue argument without adjusting for it.

**The pattern I control:** object → get dismissed → concede. Two of the three steps are mine. Worth watching in future entries more closely than his behaviour.

**Still unrecorded:**
- Has the "everyone is also working like you" line repeated since May 2025?
- What did QoreUps revenue do before vs after the Gopi split?

**Ask (what would close this):**
- **Do not raise the May 2025 remark with Bowshul.** It is ~16 months old and I agreed to the transfer afterwards. Reopening it now puts me in the wrong regardless of who was right at the time.
- Treat it as **data point #1 in a pattern**, not a live dispute.
- The live issue is #2, not this.

**Note on carrying it:** this sat unresolved for 16 months — through my own approval of the transfer — and still surfaced first when asked for an issues list. The sting outlasted the event. That points at what it represents (#2), not at Gopi.

## 2. Wants QoreUps closed — pressuring for it without saying so

**Date:** ongoing, at least since ~May 2025  ·  **Logged:** 2026-09-21
**Type:** Both — official (entity strategy) and personal (method: pressure instead of a stated position)
**Entity:** QoreUps
**Status:** Open — **this is the live issue. #1 is a symptom of it.**

**What is happening:**
QoreUps is not making good revenue. Bowshul wants it closed. He has not put that on the table as a proposal. Instead he applies pressure through resource requests — the permanent Gopi ask (#1) being the example — which hollow QoreUps out without anyone ever having to decide out loud whether it should exist.

**Basis for this reading — TO VERIFY:**
- [ ] Has Bowshul ever *said* he wants QoreUps closed? To me, to anyone, in writing?
- [ ] Or is "he wants to close QoreUps" my inference from the Gopi request and his general posture?

This distinction decides everything downstream. If he has stated it, the issue is that he is pursuing a declared goal by undeclared means. If he has not, I am acting on a hidden-motive theory that has not been tested — and a theory that conveniently relieves me of answering the revenue question myself.

**Why it matters:**
1. **The method is the breach.** A co-founder who wants an entity closed should propose closing it. Hollowing it out by transfers means the decision gets made by attrition, with no date, no numbers, and no vote. Nobody is accountable for it and it cannot be argued against.
2. **It is self-fulfilling.** Take the resources, wait for the numbers to sag, then point at the numbers. QoreUps has already lost half its manager (#1). Any future closure case built on QoreUps performance must be adjusted for what was removed from it.
3. **Gopi is a person in this.** His career is being routed by a strategy nobody has declared.

**The uncomfortable part — he may be right on substance:**
I have not written down my own answer to *should QoreUps continue?* I have been defending it because it is being taken, not because I have decided it is worth keeping. If QoreUps genuinely cannot earn, then a bad method is being used to reach a correct conclusion — and arguing only about the method lets me avoid the question. **Answer the revenue question first, separately, on numbers.**

**This also re-reads the May 2025 remark:**
"Everyone is also working like you" may not be a verdict on my work ethic. It may mean *the load you are carrying is self-inflicted — you are grinding on something that does not earn.* Still dismissive, still minimising work he can see. But that is a disagreement about whether QoreUps should exist, not about whether I work hard. Different argument, and one I can actually win or lose on evidence.

**Ask (what would close this):**
1. **Decide my own position on QoreUps** — on numbers, before any conversation with Bowshul. Revenue, cost, runway, what it would take to make it work, by when. Adjusted for the Gopi loss.
2. **Force the decision into the open.** Put QoreUps on the agenda as an explicit keep/close decision with a date. Whichever way it goes, it gets decided rather than drained.
3. **Rule for cross-entity resource moves** — who can request whom, what the receiving entity pays, who decides. Without this, entity strategy keeps getting set by whoever asks for staff.
4. **Do not frame it as an accusation.** "You want to close QoreUps and you are not saying it" is unprovable if he denies it. "QoreUps needs a keep-or-close decision and I want it made properly" gets the same outcome and cannot be deflected.
