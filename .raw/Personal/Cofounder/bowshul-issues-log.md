The presentation was prepared and never delivered as intended. A group presentation to team heads is not the same event as three separate one-to-one conversations — the preparation was wasted and the format was downgraded. And the closing line landed as the point of the whole thing.

**⚠ Evidence discipline — said vs inferred:**
