He has pushed to close **QoreUps** and **InnBlockchain**. He has never pushed to close **Trioangle**. But on the numbers Trioangle is the most distressed of the three:

**So the closure trigger he applied to InnBlockchain — a one-month salary delay — describes Trioangle far worse than it describes InnBlockchain.** Trioangle carries roughly ₹48L of accrued salary and statutory arrears. **You cannot use salary delay as grounds to close the entity with one month's delay while the entity with ₹48L of arrears is never in question.**

- I gave up, went to my own room, and ran the meeting **one-to-one with each head instead of the group PPT presentation** I had prepared.
- **After all the meetings were finished**, he came and said: *"Hey, you asked for the room — do you want it now?"*

**What he said:**
> *"Just a minute, just a minute."* (×3)
> *"Hey, you asked for the room — do you want it now?"* — after it was over

**Why it matters:**
The presentation was prepared and never delivered as intended. A group presentation to team heads is not the same event as three separate one-to-one conversations — the preparation was wasted and the format was downgraded. And the closing line landed as the point of the whole thing.

**⚠ Evidence discipline — said vs inferred:**

| | Established | Inferred by me |
|---|---|---|

**⚠ What I did not do — and it is the same thing I did not do in #1:**

**What he said:**
> *"Are you coming?"* — the day before, after everyone else had been invited properly

This is not a room booking or a dinner. It is **his wedding**, the mutual friends were all invited, and the omission was **public** — played out in the office, in front of the people closest to us, with my wife standing there unable to explain it. **The injury was not only to me and not only private.** That is what makes this categorically different from #4 and #5.

**⚠ Evidence discipline:**

| | Established | Inferred by me |
|---|---|---|
| All mutual friends + wives invited; function held at the office | ✔ | |
| I received no invitation, and did not know what the function was until that day | ✔ | |

**⚠ "Are you coming?" complicates the deliberate reading — and points at something worse:**
Asking *"are you coming?"* is what you say to someone you **assume is invited**. Deliberate exclusion followed by an open invitation the day before is incoherent as a plan.

**The reading that fits all the facts:** he never thought to invite me, and when it became visible — because our friends and their wives were all there, and mine was in the office — he patched it late. **Not malice. Absence.**

**And that is the same shape as the two entries before it:**

| Entry | Omission | Late patch |
|---|---|---|
| #4 | Would not vacate the room | *"Do you want it now?"* — after the meetings |
| #5 | Not invited to the treat | *"I forgot to call you"* — next day |
| #6 | Not invited to the function | *"Are you coming?"* — the day before |

**Three times: forgotten, then patched once visible.** A person acting out of malice does not patch. **Someone who does not hold you in mind patches when reminded.** That is a worse finding than hostility, because hostility is still a relationship.

**⚠ "If we were just friends this would not have happened — the official relationship caused it."**
**This is probably the wrong way round.** A business partnership does not make a man forget his friend at his own wedding function. The more coherent sequence is that **the friendship had already thinned, and the business is what kept us in the same room.** The partnership did not damage the friendship — **it outlasted it.**
**This matters for [[separation-plan]]:** if business killed the friendship, separation might restore it. **If the friendship was already gone, separation will not bring it back — and expecting it to is how I end up conceding terms for something that was never on offer.**

**✅ CLARIFIED 2026-09-24 — not a lost invitation, and it is worse than an omission:**
**I was invited to the marriage function. I was not invited to the pre-marriage function.**

That changes the reading materially:
- **It was not forgetfulness.** Two guest lists existed and I was on one of them. **A tiered guest list is a decision, not an absence.**
- **The pre-marriage function is the inner-circle event** — close family and closest friends. The main wedding is the broad list that everyone gets.
- **Our mutual friends and their wives were in the inner tier. I was not.** Being placed in the outer tier while the shared friend group sits in the inner one is a precise statement about where I rank.

**⚠ So for THIS entry, "absence rather than malice" is the weaker reading.** The omission-then-patch pattern still holds for #4 and #5 — but here a deliberate ranking fits the facts better than forgetting does, and *"are you coming?"* the day before reads as him registering the optics rather than remembering me.
