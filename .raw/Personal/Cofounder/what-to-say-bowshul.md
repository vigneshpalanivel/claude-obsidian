# What To Say — Bowshul

**Read before. Walk in with nothing.** Reasoning is in [[separation-plan]]; figures in [[separation-numbers-for-bowshul]].

---

## 1 · Open

> *"I want to talk about something I've been thinking about for a while."*
>
> *"I've decided I want us to separate the companies. You take Trioangle completely, I take InnBlockchain completely."*

**Then stop. Let him speak first.**

---

## READ HIM

| **RESENTFUL** | **AFRAID** |
|---|---|
| Engages on terms · pushes on money and figures · sounds relieved · moves to logistics | Goes quiet · asks how *he'll* manage · talks about how much you do · brings up the team and clients · pushes on **you staying** |
| **→ 3, 4, 5, 6** | **→ 5, then 3B, then 4, 6** |

---

## 2 · Why

> *"We've ended up wanting different things for the business, and I don't think either of us is going to change the other's mind."*
>
> *"Neither of us can afford to keep spending time and energy on the same argument. Separated, we can both move fast — you on Trioangle, me on blockchain, no clashes in the way."*
>
> *"You've got a clear idea for Trioangle — Flutter, the products, the direction you want to take it. I don't want to stand against it. You should be able to lead that without me in the way. And I'll take InnBlockchain the same way, without a barrier."*
>
> *"We've worked together a long time. I want this done smoothly — a full year of handover, whatever you need in that time. And I want us to come out of it still friends, with nothing left between us."*

---

## 3 · What he gets — RESENTFUL

> *"Trioangle never funds blockchain again. That ends."*
>
> *"You run it your way. No veto from me. Every decision yours."*
>
> *"You've been saying for years Trioangle shouldn't be carrying the other things. This is that — done properly."*

## 3B · What he gets — AFRAID

> *"Nothing changes on the ground for a year. The team stays, Ajith and Gopi stay, the clients stay. I'll be in the same office."*
>
> *"By the end of that year you'll have been running it yourself for a year already — with me there for whatever you need. The paperwork just catches up with it."*
>
> *"And Trioangle stops carrying blockchain. That ends on day one — that part you get straight away."*

**Never say *"every decision is yours"* in this version. Say *"you won't be doing it alone."***

---

## 4 · Money

> *"The auditor goes through both companies — the assets, the payables, what each of us is owed — same basis both sides."*
>
> *"Whatever the difference comes to, we settle it — whichever of us it falls on."*
>
> *"And the bank releases both our guarantees in writing."*
>
> *"Whatever the auditor says we need to do about the staff dues and the statutory side, we do — and it goes into the MoU."*
>
> *"And blockchain stays in this office for the year at no charge. After that I pay per seat, whatever the market rate is then."*
>
> *"Anything else that needs deciding, we put to the auditor and take his numbers."*

---

## 5 · Handover

> *"I'm not walking out on you. A full year — same office, whatever you need."*
>
> *"I want us to still be friends at the end of this."*

**If AFRAID, add:** *"You're not going to be sitting there on your own wondering what to do. I'll be right here."*

---

## 6 · Close

> *"Take whatever time you need. Nothing has to be decided today."*
>
> *"I'd like us to agree the shape in the next few weeks, then let the advisors do the paperwork."*

**Both sentences. The first stops him feeling cornered; the second stops it drifting.**

# IF HE PUSHES

**"Tell me what's wrong and I'll fix it"**
> *"Nothing ever got fixed before. It used to go quiet after a few days and we'd both move on — that's what we did every time. This time it didn't go quiet, and I didn't come back from it."*
>
> *"So there isn't anything sitting there for you to fix. There never was. We waited it out, and it stopped working."*

**"I'll change. I'll step back. Tell me what you need"**
> *"I believe you'd try. But it's not about what you do next — what's changed is in me. That isn't something you can fix by doing more."*

**"Is this because of me? Are you angry with me?"**
> *"No. We've been friends a long time and I'd like that to survive this. That's part of why I'd rather not turn it into a list."*

**"But what actually happened?"** — two, no more.
> *"You've told me many times that InnBlockchain should be closed. And every rupee it needed, I had to argue for. I spent years defending it instead of building it."*

> *"The AI account — I asked for the model change, and then for headroom to cut what we were burning. Neither of those landed. When the limits ran out the team lost days and I couldn't do anything about it. I'm not saying you were wrong about the models. I'm saying I stopped expecting anything I asked for to land."*

**Then:** *"None of that is fixable now. It's ten years of it."*

**If he says you should have bought more capacity:** *"Probably true. It still left me with no way to settle it."*

**Only if HE raises the workload**
> *"When I couldn't hand over even the support team — one person — I knew I was going to be carrying everything regardless."*

**One sentence, then stop. Never compare hours.**

**"Let's just carry on as we are"**
> *"That's the one thing I can't do. What's open is how and when we do it — not whether."*

**"Let's close InnBlockchain instead"**
> *"That doesn't change anything I've asked for. We'd still be partners, just in one company instead of two."*

**"You should stay at Trioangle"**
> *"I'm not leaving you stranded — a full year of handover. But me staying doesn't change what needs to change. I need the ownership separated."*

**"Let me buy you out instead"** / **"I'll buy your Trioangle shares"**
> *"That works for me too — same process. The auditor prices both, and we settle whichever way it comes out."*

**"I can't give up my InnBlockchain shares"**
> *"Can you tell me what's behind that? Is it the value, or something else?"*

**"InnBlockchain will make more money — the deals are bigger"**
> *"The deals are bigger, yes. Far fewer of them, much longer to close, and I still don't know if it works. Trioangle brings in more today than blockchain does."*
>
> *"If I were doing this for the money, I'd be asking you for Trioangle."*
>
> *"And whichever one turns out to be worth more, the auditor prices both and we settle the difference. There's nothing to gain by picking one over the other."*

**"Blockchain is better placed for the future"**
> *"You may well be right. I think it has a future, and you've told me many times it should be closed. One of us is going to turn out wrong, and whoever takes it carries that."*
>
> *"The auditor values what's on the books. Neither company's future is on the books — yours or mine. What's on the books gets valued, the difference gets settled, and after that each of us takes his own chances."*

**Anything about money — what's owed, Trioangle's debts, the figure**
> *"It's all in the auditor's numbers. Whatever he works out, we settle it — and nothing gets left hanging either way."*

**"Have you talked to anyone about this? Does anyone know?"**
> *"No one. I wanted to talk to you first, before anybody else hears anything."*

**"What about QoreUps?"**
> *"It stays as it is. Nothing's being decided — we just need someone keeping the filings current."*

**Do not defend QoreUps beyond that.**

**He brings up his own complaints**
> *"That's fair. I'm not here to argue it. What I'm proposing is…"* — one sentence, then back to the proposal.

**"I can't do this without you"** — the moment you fold. Switch to 3B.
> *"I'm not leaving you with it. I'll hand over properly for as long as it takes. But I do need the ownership separated."*

**He gets angry**
> *"I'm not trying to fight with you. Take some time and we'll talk again whenever you want."*

**Then end it.**

# RULES

1. **Never use "you" for a fault. *"You get Trioangle"* is fine. *"You didn't follow it"* is not.**
   Faults are always *"I stopped being able to"*, never *"you never listened."*
2. **Don't waive anything.** Never *"I'm not asking for the difference"* or *"take the crypto, keep my half."*
3. **Nothing final today.** Shape only.
4. **Own the years, never the figures.** *"I stayed quiet"* — yes. *"QoreUps was my mistake"* — never.
5. **Being needed is the pressure that works on you.** When it feels good to be asked to stay, say nothing and go back to the line.

---

# NEVER SAY

- *"What's the purpose of working with you?"*
- *"You don't take responsibility"* · *"you insulted me"* · anything with **toxic**
- *"You never valued my effort"* · *"I work more than you"* · *"I'll go where I get respect"*
- *"You never listen to me"* · *"you don't cooperate"*
- *"Everyone did it except Product"*
- Anything about your wife
- Anything about the QoreUps money, the old accounts, or the books

**When you feel the urge to explain everything — that is the moment to stop talking.**
