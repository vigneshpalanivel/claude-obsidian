# What To Say — Bowshul

**Read before. Walk in with nothing.** Reasoning is in [[separation-plan]]; figures in [[separation-numbers-for-bowshul]]. Neither goes into the room.

**OPEN → READ HIM → WHY → [3 or 5 first] → MONEY → CLOSE**

**Two versions of beat 3 and 5 depending on what you read. Everything else is the same.**

---

## 1 · Open

> *"I want to talk about something I've been thinking about for a while."*
>
> *"I've decided I want us to separate the companies. You take Trioangle completely, I take InnBlockchain completely."*

**Then stop. Let him speak first.**

---

## READ HIM — before you go further

**Which conversation is this? You will know inside a few minutes.**

| He is **RESENTFUL** — glad to be rid of blockchain | He is **AFRAID** — cannot run Trioangle alone |
|---|---|
| Engages on terms quickly | Goes quiet, or changes the subject |
| Pushes on money, shares, figures | Asks how *he* will manage, or how *you* managed |
| Sounds relieved | Talks about how much you do |
| Moves to logistics — when, how, who tells whom | Brings up the team, the clients, what happens to them |
| Argues about blockchain | Pushes on **you staying**, not on the money |

> **If RESENTFUL → beat 3 next.** Lead with what he gets.
> **🔴 If AFRAID → beat 5 next.** Lead with the handover. **To a frightened man, *"every decision is yours"* is a threat, not a gift — it will work against you.**

---

## 2 · Why

> *"We've ended up wanting different things for the business, and I don't think either of us is going to change the other's mind."*
>
> *"Neither of us can afford to keep spending time and energy on the same argument. Separated, we can both move fast — you on Trioangle, me on blockchain, no clashes in the way."*
>
> *"You've got a clear idea for Trioangle — Flutter, the products, the direction you want to take it. I don't want to stand against it. You should be able to lead that without me in the way. And I'll take InnBlockchain the same way, without a barrier."*
>
> *"We've worked together a long time. I want this done smoothly — a full year of handover, whatever you need in that time. And I want us to come out of it still friends, with nothing left between us."*

---

## 3 · What he gets — **if he is RESENTFUL**

> *"Trioangle never funds blockchain again. That ends."*
>
> *"You run it your way. No veto from me. Every decision yours."*
>
> *"You've been saying for years Trioangle shouldn't be carrying the other things. This is that — done properly."*

### 3B · What he gets — **if he is AFRAID.** Use this instead.

**Same content. Autonomy presented as something he grows into, not something handed to him on day one.**

> *"Nothing changes on the ground for a year. The team stays, Ajith and Gopi stay, the clients stay. I'll be in the same office."*
>
> *"By the end of that year you'll have been running it yourself for a year already — with me there for whatever you need. The paperwork just catches up with it."*
>
> *"And Trioangle stops carrying blockchain. That ends on day one — that part you get straight away."*

**⚠ Do not say *"every decision is yours"* in this version.** Say *"you won't be doing it alone."*

---

## 4 · Money

> *"The auditor works out what each company is worth and what each of us is owed — same basis both sides."*
>
> *"Whatever the difference comes to, we settle it — whichever of us it falls on."*
>
> *"And the bank releases both our guarantees in writing."*
>
> *"Whatever the auditor says we need to do about the staff dues and the statutory side, we do — and it goes into the MoU."*

---

## 5 · Handover — **if he is AFRAID, this comes before beat 3**

> *"I'm not walking out on you. A full year — same office, whatever you need."*
>
> *"I want us to still be friends at the end of this."*

**If AFRAID, add — and this is the sentence that does the work:**
> *"You're not going to be sitting there on your own wondering what to do. I'll be right here."*

**Then go to 3B, then 4.**

---

## 6 · Close

> *"Take whatever time you need. Nothing has to be decided today."*

# IF HE PUSHES

**"Tell me what's wrong and I'll fix it"**
> *"If I'd raised it at the time, maybe you could have. I didn't — for years. So there's nothing sitting there for you to fix. That window closed while I stayed quiet."*

**"I'll change. I'll step back. Tell me what you need"**
> *"I believe you'd try. But it's not about what you do next — what's changed is in me. That isn't something you can fix by doing more."*

**"Is this because of me? Are you angry with me?"**
> *"No. We've been friends a long time and I'd like that to survive this. That's part of why I'd rather not turn it into a list."*

**"But what actually happened?"** — three, no more. Say *"I'm not saying you were wrong"* with each.
> *"BMAD — I brought it in across the whole company, your team didn't use it, and I quietly dropped it rather than have the argument. Not because you were wrong about BMAD. Because I stopped being able to make anything stick."*
>
> *"When the AI limits ran out and we sent the team home for those days — I couldn't do anything about it. Not about the tokens. I had no way to settle it, and people lost work over it."*
>
> *"Twice you've told me to close InnBlockchain. I kept it going and spent years defending it instead of building it."*

**Then:** *"None of that is fixable now. It's ten years of it."*

**"Let's just carry on as we are"**
> *"That's the one thing I can't do. What's open is how and when we do it — not whether."*

**"Let's close InnBlockchain instead"**
> *"That doesn't change anything I've asked for. We'd still be partners, just in one company instead of two."*

**"You should stay at Trioangle"**
> *"I'm not leaving you stranded — a full year of handover. But me staying doesn't change what needs to change. I need the ownership separated."*

**"I can't give up my InnBlockchain shares"**
> *"Can you tell me what's behind that? Is it the value, or something else?"*

**"InnBlockchain is going to make more money — the deal sizes are bigger. That's why you're taking it, isn't it?"**
> *"The deals are bigger, yes. Far fewer of them, much longer to close, and I still don't know if it works. Trioangle brings in more today than blockchain does."*
>
> *"If I were doing this for the money, I'd be asking you for Trioangle."*
>
> *"And whichever one turns out to be worth more, the auditor prices both and we settle the difference. So there's nothing to gain by picking one over the other — that's the point of doing it this way."*

**Concede the deal sizes — he can see them, and denying it costs you everything. Then let the settlement answer the motive.**

**"Right now they're about equal. But blockchain is better placed for the future — that's what you're really taking."**
> *"You may well be right. I think it has a future, and you've told me twice it should be closed. One of us is going to turn out wrong, and whoever takes it carries that."*
>
> *"The auditor values what's on the books. Neither company's future is on the books — yours or mine. If we start pricing what each of them might become, we'll be here a year and neither of us will agree."*
>
> *"What's on the books gets valued, the difference gets settled, and after that each of us takes his own chances."*

**⚠ Do not deny it.** You do believe blockchain has the better future — that is why you are taking it. **Denying it is a lie he can see through, and it puts everything else you say in doubt.**

**⚠ Do not offer to swap** — *"if you think it's better, you take it."* It is the strongest-sounding line available and **he might accept.**

**⚠ And if he argues future value, that is good news.** He is negotiating over price, not refusing. **Price is workable.**

**Anything about money — what's owed, what Trioangle's debts are, what the figure is**
> *"It's all in the auditor's numbers. Whatever he works out, we settle it — and nothing gets left hanging either way."*

**"What about QoreUps?"**
> *"It stays as it is. Nothing's being decided — we just need someone keeping the filings current."*

**Do not defend QoreUps beyond that. Do not argue it was worth keeping.**

**He brings up his own complaints**
> *"That's fair. I'm not here to argue it. What I'm proposing is…"* — one sentence, then back to the proposal.

**He gets angry**
> *"I'm not trying to fight with you. Take some time and we'll talk again whenever you want."*

**Then end it.**

# RULES

**1 · Every sentence starts with I, never with you.**
*"I stopped being able to"* — never *"you never listened."*

**2 · Don't waive anything.**
Never *"I'm not asking for the difference"* or *"take the crypto, keep my half."* Everything goes through the auditor and gets settled.

**3 · Nothing final today.** Shape only.

**4 · Own the years, never the figures.**
*"I stayed quiet"* — yes. *"QoreUps was my mistake"* — never.

**5 · If he says *"I can't do this without you"*** — that's the moment you fold:
> *"I'm not leaving you with it. I'll hand over properly for as long as it takes. But I do need the ownership separated."*

**That sentence is also the signal: he is AFRAID. Switch to 3B if you have not already.**

**6 · Being needed is the pressure that works on you.** Not argument. When it feels good to be asked to stay, that is the moment to say nothing and go back to the line.

---

# NEVER SAY

- *"What's the purpose of working with you?"*
- *"You don't take responsibility"* · *"you insulted me"* · anything with **toxic**
- *"You never valued my effort"* · *"I work more than you"* · *"I'll go where I get respect"*
- *"You never listen to me"*
- Anything about your wife
- Anything about the QoreUps money, the old accounts, or the books

**When you feel the urge to explain everything — that is the moment to stop talking.**
