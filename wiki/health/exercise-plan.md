# Exercise Plan — Vignesh

**Equipment:** EFX elliptical + 5kg dumbbells (pair)
**Goal:** Muscle gain for BMI 17.3 → 20–21 (58.5kg → 68–70kg)
**Constraint:** Seronegative spinal/SI joint inflammation — spine-safe only. See [[profile]].

> **No double sessions (EFX + strength same day)** — at BMI 17.3 excess cardio burns muscle calories needed for gain. EFX is cortisol disposal + heart health; strength is the actual BMI lever. See [[anxiety-stress]] EFX timing rules.

> **Post-workout nutrition (non-negotiable):** eat within 30–45 min after every strength session — milk + 2 eggs is ideal. Skip this and the session doesn't build.

## Phase 1 — Weeks 1–4 (Foundation, Spine-Safe)

**Focus:** Form and control. No rushing. All bodyweight or 5kg.

### Workout A

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Cue |
|---|----------|-------------|-----|
| 1 | **Glute bridge** | 3 × 15 | Squeeze glutes at top, hold 2 sec. Protects SI joint. |
| 2 | **Dead bug** | 3 × 10/side | Lower back flat on floor the entire time — if it arches, you've gone too far |
| 3 | **Floor push-up** | 3 × 15 | Full range, chest touches floor. Hands shoulder-width. |
| 4 | **One-arm dumbbell row** (5kg, knee on chair) | 3 × 12/side | Back flat, don't rotate spine, pull elbow to hip |
| 5 | **Dumbbell bicep curl** (5kg) | 3 × 12 | Standing, controlled — no swinging at the shoulder |
| 6 | **Calf raises** (bodyweight) | 3 × 20 | Slow up, slow down — against wall for balance |

### Workout B

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Cue |
|---|----------|-------------|-----|
| 1 | **Bird dog** | 3 × 10/side | Spine neutral, slow — opposite arm + leg, hold 3 sec |
| 2 | **Glute bridge** | 3 × 15 | Same as Workout A |
| 3 | **Seated dumbbell shoulder press** (5kg) | 3 × 12 | Seated = spine supported. Press straight up, don't arch lower back |
| 4 | **Seated rear delt fly** (5kg) | 3 × 12 | Lean forward from seated, elbows out to sides — builds upper back posture |
| 5 | **Dumbbell tricep kickback** (5kg) | 3 × 12 | Hinge forward, upper arm parallel to floor, extend only the forearm |
| 6 | **Calf raises** (bodyweight) | 3 × 20 | — |

**Rest between sets:** 60–90 sec

## Phase 2 — Weeks 5–8 (Add Load)

Add these exercises into A and B. Phase 1 movements continue — drop the easier sets if session runs long.

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Cue |
|---|----------|-------------|-----|
| + | **Floor dumbbell chest press** (5kg each) | 3 × 12 | Lie on floor — safer than bench, eliminates hyperextension risk |
| + | **Goblet squat** (one 5kg dumbbell at chest) | 3 × 12 | Sit back and down, knees track over toes — front-loaded = spine-friendly |
| + | **Romanian deadlift** (5kg each hand) | 3 × 12 | Hinge at hips, soft knees, back flat — **stop immediately if SI joint complains** |
| + | **Push-up** progression | 3 × 20 | If 15 reps is easy, increase to 20 then add a 4th set |

**Progressive overload rule** (since you can't add plates):
1. Hit 20 reps × 3 sets comfortably → add a 4th set
2. 4 sets × 20 reps → reduce rest from 90 → 60 → 45 sec
3. When 5kg × 20 reps feels too easy → buy 8kg dumbbells

## ⛔ Hard Stops — Never Do With Spinal Inflammation

- Barbell squats / conventional deadlifts (heavy axial spine load)
- Standing overhead press with heavy weight
- Twisting under load: Russian twists, loaded side bends, wood chops
- Sit-ups / crunches (spinal flexion under load)
- Anything that produces SI joint or lower back pain during or after — stop, rest 2 days, then retry at lower intensity

## Daily Add-On — Kegel (Pelvic Floor) Exercises

> **Purpose:** PE management. Strongest evidence of any PE intervention — 60–80% of men see significant improvement at 4–6 weeks of daily consistency. Do this every single day, independent of the A/B strength schedule.

**How to find the muscle:** The muscle that stops urine mid-flow is the one you're targeting (PC muscle). Don't actually stop urine mid-flow as a practice — just use it once to locate it, then do the exercises dry.

| Phase | Hold | Reps | Sets | When |
|-------|------|------|------|------|
| Week 1–2 | 3 sec squeeze → 3 sec release | 10 | 3 | Morning / lunch / evening |
| Week 3–4 | 5 sec squeeze → 5 sec release | 10 | 3 | Same |
| Week 5+ | 8–10 sec squeeze → 5 sec release | 10 | 3 | Same |

**Key rules:**
- Don't hold your breath — breathe normally throughout
- Squeeze only the pelvic floor — not glutes, thighs, or abs
- Rest at least 10 sec between reps if holding long
- 100% invisible — do at desk, lying in bed, anywhere; no equipment, no gym
- **Consistency beats intensity** — 3 sets/day every day beats 10 sets once in a while
- 4–6 weeks before noticeable effect; 8–12 weeks for full benefit

**Quick sessions (2 min each):**
- Morning: 10 reps × 3 sec hold while having breakfast
- Afternoon: 10 reps × 5 sec hold at desk (no one knows)
- Pre-sleep: 10 reps × 8 sec hold lying in bed

> See [[anxiety-stress]] PE section for the full management approach (stop-start technique, parasympathetic breathing, Dr. Sabrin consultation on dapoxetine/SSRI if Kegels alone aren't enough after 3 months).

## Tracking Progress

Weigh weekly (same as [[meal-log]] weigh-in). For strength, track:
- Push-up max reps per set
- Whether Phase 2 exercises feel easier (drop in perceived effort = real gain)

No need to log every session — just note when you move from Phase 1 → 2 → 3.
