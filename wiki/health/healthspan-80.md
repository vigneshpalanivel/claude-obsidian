Active and independent at **80 years old (year 2072)**, defined concretely as:

> This is a **healthspan** target, not a lifespan target. The goal is not to reach 80 — it is to reach 80 still driving your own life.

## The Core Thesis

**Fitness, muscle, and bone are depreciating assets. You cannot buy them late. You can only bank them early and defend the balance.**

Three facts set the whole timeline:

| Asset | Peaks at | Decline rate | Implication for Vignesh (age 34) |
|-------|----------|--------------|----------------------------------|
| **Muscle mass** | Age 30–35 | ~1–2%/yr after 50, ~3%/yr after 60 | **You are inside your peak window RIGHT NOW and underweight at BMI 17.4.** This is the most time-sensitive fact in your entire health picture. |
| **VO2max** | Age 25–30 | ~10%/decade after 30, faster after 70 | To have "basic cardio" at 80, you need genuinely trained cardio at 45. Currently zero aerobic development in the plan. |
| **Bone density** | Age ~30 | ~0.5–1%/yr after 40 (men) | Peak already passed. BMI 17.4 + chronically low Vit D + no weight-bearing history = you likely started below average. Never measured. |

**Muscle protein synthesis efficiency falls with age (anabolic resistance).** Building 1 kg of lean mass at 60 takes several times the effort it takes at 34. Every kg you build in the next 5 years is banked for the next 45. Every kg you don't build is permanently forgone.

> **This is why [[bmi-increase-plan]] is not a cosmetic weight-gain plan. It is the single most important milestone in this entire document, and it has a closing window.**

**The primary determinant of independence at 80.** Grip strength and leg strength predict all-cause mortality better than almost any other single measure.

**Governing file:** [[bmi-increase-plan]] → executed via [[exercise-plan]] → tracked in [[exercise-log]] + [[meal-log]]

**Non-negotiables:**

- Never lose weight deliberately. Ever. Your risk direction is underweight, not over.

### Pillar 2 — Cardiorespiratory Fitness (VO2max)

**Currently the largest structural gap.** [[exercise-plan]] explicitly assigns EFX to cortisol disposal, not aerobic training — correct for today, but with no planned exit, "minimal cardio" silently becomes permanent.

**Why cardio cannot start now:** it burns the surplus that [[bmi-increase-plan]] depends on. Sequencing matters more than enthusiasm.

| Phase | Weight gate | Cardio role | Target |
|-------|------------|-------------|--------|
| **A — Suppressed** | 59 → 65 kg | Cortisol disposal only | EFX 25 min × 2/wk, resistance 1–2 |
| **B — Base building** | 65 → 70 kg | Aerobic base enters the plan | Zone 2: 30–45 min conversational pace, 2–3×/wk |
| **C — Development** | 70 kg+ | VO2max becomes co-priority | Zone 2 base + 1 weekly high-intensity block |
| **D — Lifelong** | Age 40+ | Permanent | 150+ min/wk moderate, VO2max tested every 2–3 yrs |

**The arithmetic:** to hold ~25 ml/kg/min at 80 (comfortably above the ~18 independence floor), you need roughly 45–50 at age 40–45. That does not happen by accident.

**Immediate:** EFX progression 11 → 14 → 17 → 20 → 25 min (currently at 14, [[exercise-log]]).

## Balance Training — Missing and Free

Falls are the leading cause of injury death in over-65s and the single largest travel risk in old age. Currently absent from [[exercise-plan]] entirely.

**Add to the end of every strength session — 2 minutes:**

| Exercise | Duration | Progression |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| Single-leg stand, eyes open | 30 sec/side | → eyes closed |
| Tandem stance (heel-to-toe) | 30 sec | → eyes closed |
| Heel-to-toe walk | 10 steps | → head turns while walking |

Costs nothing, takes 2 minutes, and is one of the highest-ROI additions for the world-travel goal specifically.

## The Five Rules That Decide the Outcome

1. **Never lose weight deliberately.** Your risk direction is underweight, for life. Low BMI in the elderly is a mortality risk, not a virtue.
2. **Resistance training never stops.** Not a phase, not a project — a permanent 3×/week fixture until the day you can't. This is the independence lever.
3. **Sleep is the highest-value intervention you have.** It compounds into brain, muscle recovery, cortisol, and immune function simultaneously. Protect the 10 PM–6 AM window like a business commitment.
4. **Fix root causes, don't accumulate management protocols.** The allergen test, the DEXA, the Sulfasalazine question — each closes a loop instead of adding a daily workaround. A life with 6 food restrictions at 34 becomes 12 at 60 unless you go upstream.
5. **Measure on interval, not on symptom.** Every 🔴 in the dashboard above exists because nobody ordered a test. Symptoms are a lagging indicator; on a 46-year horizon they arrive too late to act on.
