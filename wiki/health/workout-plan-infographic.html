A and B alternate continuously — neither is tied to a weekday. The sequence just runs A · B · A · B · A · B … through the Mon / Wed / Fri slots, so the rotation flips every week. Track which one you did last, not what day it is — if a session is missed, the next strength day continues the sequence rather than resetting to A. Each workout therefore averages 1.5×/week, not 2.

WORKOUT A

alternates with B · ~42 min · avg 1.5×/week
Block 1 · Push / Pull superset — no rest between the pair, 60–90 s after
Floor push-upSUPERSET
3 × 15
Chest touches floor. Progress the 6-level ladder.
Inverted row — underhandNEW
3 × 10
Bar at hip–waist height, not lower. Underhand, elbows tucked = lats lead. Feet further out = harder.
Block 2 · Chest + Arm superset
Floor dumbbell flySUPERSET
3 × 12 · 3 kg
Wide shallow arc, slight elbow bend. Floor stops the stretch.
Bicep curl
3 × 12 · 3 kg
Standing, controlled. No swinging at the shoulder.
Block 3 · Leg block — real sets, 90 s rest. This is the thigh work.
Step-upSUPERSET
3 × 12 / side
Step 30–40 cm. Lower over 4 seconds — the slow descent is the point.
Hamstring slider curlNEW
3 × 12
Heels on a towel. Hips stay up throughout. Only direct hamstring work in the plan.
Wall sitSUPERSET
2 × 45–60 s
Back flat, thighs parallel. At 60 s, switch to single-leg — don't just add time.
Single-leg calf raise
2 × 15 / side
Full range, slow lowering. Wall for balance.
Block 4 · Upper back finisher — the only lower-trap work in the plan
Prone Y-T-W raiseNEW
2 × 8 each position
Face down, forehead on a towel, chest stays on the floor. Lift arms only: Y (overhead 45°), T (straight out), W (elbows bent, blades squeezed down). Chest off the floor = this has become a superman, which is banned.
Block 5 · Core — anti-movement only
Dead bug
2 × 10 / side
Lower back stays flat on the floor. If it arches, shorten the range.
Side plank
2 × 20–30 s / side
Knees first. Hips stacked and lifted — a dropped hip trains nothing.
Bird dog
2 × 10 / side
Opposite arm + leg, hold 3 s. Spine neutral — balance a glass on your back.
Balance block
2 min
See DAILY below. Never cut — Pillar 1, fall risk.

WORKOUT B

alternates with A · ~44 min · avg 1.5×/week
Block 1 · Vertical push / pull superset
Seated shoulder pressSUPERSET
3 × 12 · 3 kg
Seated = spine supported. Press straight up, don't arch the lower back.
Pull-up negativesNEW
3 × 5
Stop parking on dead hangs — ~18 days at L1–2 gave no lat stimulus. Step to the top, lower over 5 seconds. Neutral or underhand grip — never wide overhand.
Block 2 · Horizontal pull — mid-back focus
Inverted row — wide overhand
3 × 10
Body one straight braced line. Wide grip, elbows flared = rhomboids / mid-traps lead, vs the underhand lat version in Workout A.
Block 3 · Upper accessory superset
Seated rear delt flySUPERSET
3 × 12 · 3 kg
Lean forward from seated, elbows out. Builds upper-back posture.
Tricep kickback
3 × 12 · 3 kg
Upper arm stays parallel to floor. Only the forearm moves.
Block 4 · Hip circuit — 2 rounds, 15 s between stations
Glute bridge
15
Squeeze glutes at the top, hold 2 s.
Clamshell
15 / side
Feet together, lift top knee. Directly stabilises the SI joint.
Single-leg glute bridge
10 / side
One leg extended. More glute/hamstring, zero spine stress.
Side-lying leg adduction
15 / side
Bottom leg raises straight. Inner thigh. Hips stacked, no rolling.
Prone hip extension
12 / side
Face down, leg lifts straight, hold 1 s. Squeeze the glute, not the back.
Block 5–6 · Forearms superset + finisher
Wrist curlSUPERSET
2 × 15 · 3 kg
Forearm on thigh, palm up, hand past the knee. Curl the wrist only.
Reverse wrist curl
2 × 15 · 3 kg
Same position, palm down. Trains the neglected extensors.
Single-leg calf raise
2 × 15 / side
Slow lowering. Wall for balance.

Balance · 2 min — Pillar 1, fall risk. Wall within arm's reach.
Single-leg stand
30 s / side
Hips level, eyes on a fixed point. Progress to eyes closed.
Tandem stance
30 s
Heel touching toe, like a tightrope. Switch front foot halfway.
Heel-to-toe walk
10 steps
Heel directly against the other toe. Slow. Wall nearby, don't lean.
Pelvic floor (3× daily)
slow holds + flicks
Morning · afternoon · pre-sleep. Invisible — do it anywhere.

Non-negotiables

  • Warmup before every session — 14-yr left shoulder
  • Rows must be supported — never free bent-over
  • Eat within 30–45 min after: milk + 2 eggs
  • Rest: none in superset · 60–90 s after pair · 90 s after leg pairs
  • Dumbbells in both hands — never one side only
  • No double sessions — EFX and strength on separate days
  • EFX seated, with 3–5 min standing broken into short blocks

Hard stops — never

  • Barbell squats · conventional deadlifts
  • Sit-ups · crunches · Russian twists
  • Hanging leg raises · toes-to-bar
  • Kipping / swinging pull-ups
  • Supermans · back extensions — anti-movement only
  • Wide overhand pull-ups as a starting grip
  • Any SI joint or lower-back pain → stop, rest 2 days, retry lighter
